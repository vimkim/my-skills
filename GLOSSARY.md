# Personal skills collections

The language used to distinguish collections, installed skills, and synchronization.

## Language

**Collection**:
A maintained group of skills distributed together, such as my-skills or my-cubrid-skills.

**Installed skill**:
The copy of a collection's skill available for an agent to use.

**Collection sync**:
Reconciliation of a collection's installed skills with its current contents, including additions, changes, and removal of obsolete skills belonging to that collection.
_Avoid_: Install/reinstall when referring to the complete reconciliation.

**Migration**:
Transfer of a skill from one collection to another while preserving its availability to users.
